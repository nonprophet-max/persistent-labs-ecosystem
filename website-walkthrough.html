Persistent Labs/FireFlow

FireFlow

AI orchestration for regulated financial operations.

FireFlow coordinates agents, tools and workflow steps within the structure your institution defines. Build the process around permitted actions, human review and the records your teams need to understand execution.

Book a FireFlow demo ↗Compare orchestration versions →
Full orchestration

Design the workflow.
Define the boundaries.

Bring specialized agents and connected systems together around a defined operational task.

01

Build workflows visually

Connect reusable steps into a process your team can inspect and refine. Build workflows around the systems and agents your task requires.

02

Place review where it matters

Make approval a deliberate part of the workflow. Separate the work an agent prepares from the action an authorized person approves.

03

Carry work across interruptions

Use durable execution to preserve workflow progress and support recovery. Define recovery behavior alongside the systems involved.

04

Keep the execution record

Inspect recorded steps, inputs and outputs. Give operations and technical teams a shared basis for investigation.

An illustrative workflow

Prepare a payment.
Put review in the process.

An agent gathers the payment details. The workflow checks the required information and routes the proposal for review. An authorized user approves or rejects it.

Connected payment systems and authorization policies determine the production implementation.
